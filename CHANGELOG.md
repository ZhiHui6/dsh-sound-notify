## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added

- Sound notifications for task completion, task failure, and standalone agent errors.
- Nine built-in tones synthesised with the Web Audio API, with nothing downloaded.
- Custom audio files up to 1.5 MB each, stored in browser local storage.
- Per-event sound mapping, volume control, master switch, and sub-agent inclusion.
- Instant preview for every sound, plus a test playback action.
- Bilingual settings page, English and Chinese.
